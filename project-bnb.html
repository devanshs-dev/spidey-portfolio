FIG. 06

Project on B&B

Market research and partnership strategy for independent bed-and-breakfast operators looking to compete with larger listing platforms.

ROLE
Consultant, 180 Degrees Consulting
TIMELINE
6 weeks
TYPE
Strategy / Research
STATUS
Completed

The Problem

Independent B&B operators were losing bookings to aggregator platforms that captured most of the customer relationship and margin.

The Approach

  • Ran competitor and market analysis across regional B&B and aggregator platforms
  • Analyzed booking and pricing data to identify under-served customer segments
  • Proposed a direct-booking and local partnership strategy to reduce platform dependency
  • Presented findings and recommendations to stakeholders as a formal deliverable

The Outcome

Delivered a strategy report with concrete partnership and pricing recommendations adopted for the operator's next planning cycle.
