Student Risk Dashboard

An XGBoost classifier that flags at-risk students early, wired into a live dashboard built for faculty with no ML background.

ROLE
ML Intern, IIT BHU
TIMELINE
10 weeks
TYPE
ML + Dashboard
STATUS
Completed

The Problem

Faculty had academic and attendance data spread across spreadsheets with no early-warning signal for students likely to underperform or drop out.

The Approach

  • Cleaned and merged multi-semester academic, attendance, and engagement records
  • Engineered features capturing trend, not just snapshot performance
  • Trained and tuned an XGBoost classifier, benchmarked against logistic regression and random forest baselines
  • Built a dashboard translating model output into plain-language risk tiers for non-technical staff
  • Validated predictions against historical outcomes to check for false positives

The Outcome

Delivered a working risk-scoring pipeline faculty could act on directly, without needing to interpret raw model scores.
