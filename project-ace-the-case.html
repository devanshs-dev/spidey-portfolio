The Brief

Acquisition Analysis and Strategies for BharatMart — a live case competition requiring structured business analysis and a presentable recommendation within a tight turnaround, run in teams.

Leading the Team

As Team Lead, my role was coordinating a 7-person team across research, analysis, and design under competition time pressure — assigning workstreams, keeping the team aligned on the case narrative, and pulling the final deliverable together.

  • Ishan — Business Analyst
  • Jhanvi — Research Analyst
  • Ayush — Market Analyst

  • Himanshu — Content Head
  • Kirthi — Design Co-Lead

What I Took From It

Running point on a team this size under competition constraints was less about the case content and more about process — keeping seven people moving in the same direction, resolving disagreements fast, and making sure the final deck actually told one coherent story instead of seven disconnected parts.
